## 17. Product questions (all decided) and residual risks

### 17.1 Product questions (genuine trade-offs, each with its options and a recommendation, all decided)

**No question in this section is open.** The owner answered **Q13 to Q34 on 2026-09-17, each with the recommended option**, and on the same day issued a **standing decision**: every product question in this section, _including any question a later review round adds_, is decided as its recommended option. Every row below is therefore a decision, treated like D1 to D4 — Q1 to Q12 from the v1 merge (§17.1.3), Q13 to Q34 answered individually (§17.1.2), and Q35 to Q38 proposed by reviewers in rounds 4 and 5 (§17.1.1). Each row keeps its options and its recommendation as they stood, so the trade-off stays auditable. A finding can reopen a decision only by showing a concrete defect — a failure scenario with evidence — and its fix must either keep the decision or propose a **new** question with a recommendation, which the standing decision then settles the same way. v6 did that twice: BA-r5-03 corrected Q9's _rationale_ while keeping its answer, and SEC-r5-01 became the new Q38 `[R5]`.
