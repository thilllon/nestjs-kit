## How to read this document

**Provenance tags.** Every design element names the draft(s) it came from: `[A]`, `[B]`, `[C]`, `[L]` for a change the lead made during the v1 merge, `[R1:<finding id>]` for a change made in response to review round 1 (for example `[R1:SEC-r1-01]`) and `[R2:<finding id>]` for a change made in response to review round 2 (for example `[R2:BA-r2-06]`), `[R3:<finding id>]` for a change made in response to review round 3 (for example `[R3:NEST-r3-01]`), `[R4:<finding id>]` for a change made in response to review round 4 (for example `[R4:SEC-r4-01]`), and `[R5:<finding id>]` for a change made in response to review round 5 (for example `[R5:NEST-r5-01]`). A bare `[R1]` to `[R5]` marks a table row or paragraph whose finding ids are given inline, and `[R1:<reviewer> decision]` to `[R5:<reviewer> decision]` marks a change that answers a reviewer's proposed user decision rather than a finding (§17.1). `[R2:<reviewer> observation on <finding id>]` to `[R5:<reviewer> observation on <finding id>]`, and the short form `[R5:<reviewer> observation]`, mark a change that answers an observation a reviewer attached to the status of an earlier finding (the ledger's prior-finding table); an observation is not a finding and has no id of its own. The decision log (section 16) records the alternatives rejected and why; the review ledger (`ledger.md`) records every finding and its resolution. `v1` to `v6` always mean design versions; library releases are written `1.0` and `1.x`.
