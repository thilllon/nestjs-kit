Add the `nestjs-aspect` package for Spring-style aspect-oriented programming in NestJS. `AspectModule.forRoot()` and `forRootAsync()` weave aspects into every class the Nest container manages (providers, controllers, enhancers and middleware of every scope) before the router binds route handlers and before the first lifecycle hook, with the `returnValueAdapters`, `report`, `strict` and `exclude` options, bootstrap validation and a weaving report. The package provides typed annotations (`createAnnotation`), the `P` pointcut designators with `and`, `or` and `not` composition, the `@Aspect`, `@Before`, `@After`, `@AfterReturning`, `@AfterThrowing` and `@Around` decorators with advice signatures checked against their pointcut, the `@Advisor` decorator with the `PointcutAdvisor` and `MethodInterceptor` interfaces, the `AdviceAdapter` and `ReturnValueAdapter` extension interfaces with the built-in `promiseAdapter`, and the `AspectConfigurationError`, `AspectUsageError` and `AspectNotReadyError` error classes.
